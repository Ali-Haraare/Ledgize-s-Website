Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ledgize ("Ledgize," "we," "us") collects through ledgize.com (the "Site"), how it is used, and the choices you have. This page covers only this website; it does not cover the separate systems used to deliver bookkeeping and advisory services to engaged clients, which are governed by your signed engagement agreement.

Information we collect

The Site collects information only when you choose to provide it, specifically through the contact form. That may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The e-commerce platforms you sell on, if you tell us
  • Any additional message you write in the form

The contact form does not submit this information to a server or database. Submitting the form opens your own email application with a pre-filled message addressed to ali@ledgize.com; the information is sent only if and when you actually send that email through your own email account.

Analytics

The Site may use a cookie-less, privacy-focused analytics tool (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic, like how many people visit and which pages they view. This type of analytics does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not collect personally identifiable information.

How we use information

Any information you send us through the contact form or by email is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Schedule and prepare for a discovery call or diagnostic audit, if requested
  • Maintain records related to prospective and active client relationships

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

Data retention

Since the Site itself does not store form submissions, retention of any information you send us is governed by standard email retention practices in the mailbox that receives it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under regimes such as the GDPR in the UK/EU or applicable privacy laws in Australia, Canada, and the United States), you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information you've sent us. To exercise any of these rights, email us at ali@ledgize.com.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ali@ledgize.com.
