The Problem

Your books say one thing. Your bank account says another.

Most multi-channel sellers aren't short on data — they're short on numbers they can trust.

Margins that hide the truth

QuickBooks or Xero shows a number — but it isn't net of marketplace fees, ad spend, and returns. The "profit" on your P&L rarely matches what actually lands in the bank.

Books that don't reconcile

Amazon settlements, Shopify payouts, and eBay fees each hit your bank on their own schedule, in their own lump sums — leaving books that are technically "done" but not actually accurate.

No real-time read on cash

By the time month-end close tells you where you stand, the decisions that mattered — inventory buys, ad budget, hiring — already happened without that information.
