The Problem

Margins that hide the truth

QuickBooks Online shows a number, but it isn't net of marketplace fees, ad spend, and returns. The "profit" on your P&L rarely matches what actually lands in the bank.

Books that don't reconcile

Amazon settlements and Shopify payouts each hit your bank on their own schedule, in their own lump sums, leaving books that are technically "done" but not actually accurate.
