What this shows, and what it doesn't

  • The maths is exact; the answer is only as good as the numbers you enter. Most sellers don't have clean per-channel figures, which is exactly the problem good books solve.
  • Use the product cost of what you sold, not what you bought this month. Stock you bought but haven't sold isn't a cost yet.
  • Leave sales tax out of sales. It's money you're holding for the states, not income.
  • This isn't your net profit. It stops before overheads like software, staff, rent and bookkeeping, which aren't tied to one channel.
  • Ads are only as accurate as how you split them. Meta and Google ads usually drive Shopify; Amazon ads drive Amazon.
